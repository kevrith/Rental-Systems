7. Owner's Obligations

The Owner warrants that they are entitled to let the properties, shall keep them insured, shall meet all rates, land rent and statutory outgoings, and shall put the Agent in funds where authorised expenditure exceeds rent collected in any month.

8. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving {{ agreement.notice_period_days }} days' written notice to the other. On termination the Agent shall render a final statement, remit all money held for the Owner less any fee properly due, and hand over all tenancy agreements, deposit records, keys and correspondence. Tenancies granted by the Agent before termination survive it and bind the Owner.

9. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Kenya. It is executed electronically, and the parties agree that an electronic signature verified by one-time password has the same effect as a manuscript signature under the Kenya Information and Communications Act.
